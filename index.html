What Schoool covers

The school day, in one place

Attendance

Mark it in seconds. When a student is absent, the parent is told on WhatsApp.

Fees

Reminders go out before and after the due date. A receipt follows as soon as the payment is recorded.

Exams

Timetables and results reach parents. Marksheets can be sent and printed.

Student records

One profile from admission onward, including certificates the school needs to print.

Accounts

Income and expenses sit with the rest of the school, with a Tally-compatible export.

Staff

Roles, leave, and a check-in for the people who run the school day.

How onboarding works

More than 150 schools are already running on it. A new school follows the same three steps.

Pick a plan

The school confirms the plan. Setup starts the same day.

Send the data

Students, classes, the fee structure, and staff. The Schoool team loads it.

Open the school

Within a week the school is working in Schoool, including the WhatsApp messages parents already know how to read.
