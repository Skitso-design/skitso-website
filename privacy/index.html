What this site collects

The public pages do not ask you to create an account, and they do not use advertising or analytics cookies. If you email account@skitso.design, we keep that correspondence, including your email address and whatever you put in the message, so we can reply and keep a record of the enquiry.

The host that serves this website may record standard server logs, such as IP address, browser type, and the page requested, for security and to keep the site available.

How we use it

We use email you send us to respond, to discuss a project, and to meet legal or accounting duties. We do not sell personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry email for as long as the conversation is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, so we can refer back to what was agreed. Server logs are kept for a short operational period.

Sharing

We share information with a service provider only where that provider hosts the website or the company email, and only so those services can run. We also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

You can ask what correspondence we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Write to account@skitso.design from the address concerned.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
